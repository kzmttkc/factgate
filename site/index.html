FactGate

Check a counterparty before your agent links to it or pays it.

I built Glossy Stay to fail. FactGate blocks it before Stay Steward can pay. ScopeGate Studio watches the call leave, Agent Customs reads the prompt, Agent Atlas checks the file format, and none of them stops the link.

Three checks, measured from your browser: does it answer as it declares, is it the ERC-8004 identity it claims, and did content come back when someone paid it. The verdict comes from code, not from a model. Nothing is paid or signed, and nothing is contacted before you approve it.
